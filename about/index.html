My Story. Why I Do This.

    The Awakening

  • ☸
    The Unraveling

    I let go of everything that no longer fit who I was becoming — including every friend.

  • ♛
    The Rebuild

    I devoted myself to unprogramming my mind, regulating my body, and recreating my identity from the inside out.

    The Return

    I became the woman I once dreamed of being — and now I help other women do the same.
